# Treasury

## Funding Source

The Magnet Strategies treasury is funded through revenue generated by Bazooka Labs'
applications, allocated to the treasury over time. This provides a consistent, growing
capital base without relying on token sales, inflationary emissions, or community
fundraising.

---

## Role

The treasury is a **liquidity-deployment engine**, not a $U accumulator. Capital is
deployed into Algorand DeFi — primarily by acquiring a partner token and pairing it
with $U in a DEX liquidity pool, building market depth around the token. It may also
back protocol reserves (e.g. USDC in the MagnetFi PSM) and other vetted strategies.

Deployment mechanics for a $U-paired pool:

1. Treasury capital acquires the target project's token.
2. That token is paired with $U in a liquidity pool.
3. The pool is deployed on the DEX best suited to the pair (Tinyman, Pact, …).

---

## Direction & Oversight

- **UVote** — where treasury liquidity is pointed is a frequent subject of UVote
  proposals; $U holders signal a preferred direction (advisory). See [UVOTE.md](../vote/UVOTE.md).
- **Founder oversight** — deployment is executed by the founder, who retains final
  authority to avoid pointing capital at unacceptable risk, regardless of vote outcome.
- **Visibility** — the treasury's live USDC balance is shown on the `/vote` page, so
  voters can see the pool a decision is directing.

---

## Transparency

All treasury deployments are on-chain and publicly verifiable on Algorand — pool
positions, balances, and transaction history are accessible at any time via on-chain
data (e.g. allo.info) or the relevant DEX interface.
